SFXVault deployment
1. Upload all files/folders in this ZIP to the ROOT of your GitHub repository.
2. Vercel should detect Next.js automatically.
3. In Vercel Settings -> Environment Variables add:
   DATABASE_URL = your Neon connection string
   BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN = your Vercel Blob token
4. Redeploy.
5. Open the site and use the Upload section.
Do not upload .env or secrets to GitHub.
